PREDICTIVE DIGITAL TWIN

Forecasting Urban Transportation Networks with Spatio-Temporal Graph Neural Networks

// PROJECT_OVERVIEW

Urban traffic congestion is a critical challenge, leading to delays, pollution, and economic loss. This project introduces a Predictive Digital Twin for the Los Angeles highway network—a dynamic virtual replica that not only mirrors the current traffic state but also forecasts future conditions with high accuracy.

By leveraging advanced Spatio-Temporal Graph Neural Networks (STGNNs), our framework learns the complex, interconnected patterns of traffic flow across both space (the road network) and time. The result is a powerful analytical tool that moves beyond reactive traffic management to enable proactive, data-driven decision-making.

Explore the live interface to visualize forecasts, simulate "what-if" scenarios like accidents or road closures, and see AI-driven route optimization in action.
